What it is, and what it isn't

What it is

A working demo of one rule: essential money stays essential. You set the split. You see what is safe to spend each day until the next loan payment. The demo declines a purchase that would blow the fun-money pot.

What it isn't

A bank, a real card, or financial advice. Nothing is imposed by a university or the Student Loans Company. You stay in control — you can decline any request yourself.

Where it's at

Built independently. Shaped so far by feedback from a small number of UK students. If the idea holds up, the next step is connecting to real accounts (Open Banking or a partner). This demo exists to find out whether the hard split actually changes behaviour.

The contrast

Without a hard split — many students hit £0 with weeks still left in the term.
With the split — essential money stays ring-fenced; you only spend from the discretionary pot.

How to try it

About two minutes. One device is enough — switch between Till and Wallet Card. Two devices give the live handoff. Every visit gets its own private session.

  1. 1Open the demo

    Start on Wallet Card and set your next loan date if you want. Then switch to Till (the shop), scan a large spending-money item, and request payment.

  2. 2Pair a second device (optional)

    Scan the QR or enter the session code on your phone. That device becomes the Wallet Card where you approve or decline. One device still works — just toggle modes at the top.

  3. 3Feel the decline

    Approve a small essential purchase, then try a large spending-money one. Watch the warning or the automatic decline when it exceeds the pot. That is the point of the demo.

Only one device? Switch between Till and Wallet Card on the same screen. You still see the safe-to-spend figure and the decline — you only miss the live handoff between two screens.

What's in the demo

Safe to spend each day

Enter your next loan payment date and an optional bill (e.g. rent). BAV reserves the bill, then shows what is safe to spend each day from the rest.

A second tap for spending money

Essentials need one tap. Spending-money purchases need a second, deliberate tap — with a clear nudge when it would take a big slice of what is left.

You stay in control

Decline any request yourself. After three declines in a row the card freezes until you unlock it — you are never locked out by someone else.

Optional trusted-contact alerts

Choose someone to be told about larger spending-money purchases. It is off by default, and in the demo it is simulated, so nobody is actually contacted.

What happens to my details?

What is collected: your first name, your email address, and anything else you choose to add (your university or college, and how you describe yourself).

Who holds it: BAV is run independently by Blacksand Designs, based in South Africa. Details are stored with Google Firebase on servers in the European Union (Belgium).

How long: tester details are deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.
