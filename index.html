How to feel the point in 2 minutes
  1. Set your next loan date (or keep the example) on the Wallet Card.
  2. Switch to Till, scan a big discretionary item (headphones, trainers, night out), request payment.
  3. On the Wallet Card, try to pay — watch the warning or the automatic decline.

One device is enough: toggle Wallet Card ↔ Till at the top. Two devices (laptop + phone) give the live handoff.

How is my security protected?
This demo never touches a real bank account. If BAV became real: no bank logins or passwords would ever be stored by BAV. Access would go through UK Open Banking — a regulated standard that many UK banks and apps already use, designed for read-only, revocable access — never screen-scraping or password storage. You could revoke access at any time from your own bank's app.
